Research Interests

My research interests center on sequential decision-making under uncertainty. My current work in Bayesian optimization and Gaussian process bandits examines how to learn useful models from limited, noisy data and when acquiring more information is worth its cost. More broadly, I am interested in adaptive experimentation and resource allocation in AI, scientific discovery, and business operations.

Research Papers

ALAS: Additive Learnable Alpha-Stable Kernels for Flexible Bayesian Optimization

Weibo Huang, Cheng Hua

ICML 2026

We developed a GP kernel family that learns effective smoothness from data, helping Bayesian optimization adapt to unknown objective structure. Information-gain and regret guarantees connect the kernel’s spectral behavior to optimization performance. Experiments demonstrate robust performance across smooth, rough, and oscillatory benchmarks.

Paper arXiv Code Poster

Gaussian Process Bandits with Paired Auxiliary Observations

Weibo Huang, Qing Feng, Ruihao Zhu, Cheng Hua

AI-GPB exploits correlated noise in paired observations to make expensive evaluations more informative, without requiring the auxiliary mean to approximate the objective. Our analysis accounts for the persistent bias introduced by estimating this mean from offline data.

arXiv PDF

Learning Preference Distributions for Diverse Pareto Fronts via Decision-dependent Bilevel Optimization

Qilong Wu, Zhihao Gu, Weibo Huang, Junchi Yang

Manuscript under review

This work jointly learns a hypernetwork and a preference-sampling distribution to produce diverse trade-off solutions in multiobjective optimization. The approach uses decision-dependent bilevel optimization, with convergence guarantees for a first-order penalty algorithm.

Ongoing Research

Cost-Aware Bayesian Optimization

Joint work with Qian Xie

We study the tradeoff between evaluation cost and final solution quality. We established expected-budget guarantees for fixed-parameter Pandora’s Box Gittins-index policies and anytime regret bounds for the adaptive PBGI-D variant.

Global Exploration and Local Refinement in Bayesian Optimization

With guidance from L. Jeff Hong and Cheng Hua

I am developing a two-level approach to high-dimensional Bayesian optimization, combining global GP-guided exploration with local refinement. The work studies how local smoothness and information retained from previous searches can improve the allocation of a limited evaluation budget.
